METU Signals Lab

Signal Processing Research Group, Middle East Technical University

About the Group

The METU Signals Lab is a small research group in the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Middle East Technical University. Our work focuses on direction-of-arrival (DOA) estimation for coherent signals using sparse arrays.

We study how classical model-based methods and modern deep learning approaches can be combined to improve estimation performance in array signal processing.
